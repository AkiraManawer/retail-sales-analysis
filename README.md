# Critical analysis questions and answes
Q1. Why can the best-selling product by quantity differ from the highest-earning product?

The best-selling product by quantity was Rooibos Tea 100G, with 570 units sold.

However, the highest-earning product was Coffee Beans 1Kg, which generated R77,760.00 in revenue.

This shows that the product with the highest number of units sold does not necessarily generate the highest revenue.

Revenue depends on both the number of units sold and the selling price of the product. Therefore, a product can sell fewer units but generate more revenue if it has a higher selling price.

Q2. Is the highest-revenue category leading because it sells the most units or because its products cost more?

The highest-revenue category was Beverages, with revenue of R109,320.00.

Beverages sold 1,009 units, which was not the highest quantity. Dairy sold the most units, with 1,054 units.

The average unit price for Beverages was R108.51, while the average unit price for Dairy was R63.80.

Meat had the highest average unit price at R127.57, but it sold only 820 units.

Therefore, Beverages generated the highest revenue because it combined a relatively high average unit price with a high number of units sold.

Its 1,009 units were close to the highest quantity sold, while its average unit price of R108.51 was much higher than Dairy, Produce and Bakery.

This shows that both sales volume and price contribute to category revenue.

Q3. Which store would you invest in and why? What extra information would you want before committing?

Sandton generated the highest revenue, with R89,137.50.

It sold 1,225 units, which was almost the same quantity as Pretoria, which sold 1,226 units. Pretoria generated R84,412.00 in revenue.

Rosebank generated the lowest revenue at R67,184.50 and also had the lowest quantity sold at 1,011 units.

The sales data shows that Sandton generated the highest revenue in this dataset. However, revenue alone is not enough to determine whether an investment would be profitable.

Before making an investment decision, additional information would be needed, including:

Operating costs for each store
Profit and profit margin
Rent and other overhead costs
Number of customers and customer growth
Average transaction value by store
Stock and inventory costs
Staff costs
Store size and capacity
Local market and competition
Sales trends over a longer period

This information would help determine whether the higher revenue at Sandton also results in higher profitability.

Q4. What does the monthly revenue trend suggest, and what should the business investigate?

Monthly revenue changed significantly during the period analysed.

Revenue increased from R44,140.00 in January to a peak of R77,000.00 in May.

Revenue then decreased to R58,026.50 in June before falling sharply to R2,728.00 in July.

The large decrease in July should be investigated.

The business should investigate:

Number of transactions in July
Quantity sold in July
Individual store performance in July
Product availability
Possible stock shortages
Whether July contains a complete month of data
Whether there were any data-entry problems

The dataset does not provide enough information to determine the exact reason for the July decline, so further investigation would be required before drawing a conclusion.
